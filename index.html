01 / Company

A Hong Kong biotech company

SO Organic Biotech Enterprise Limited (“SOBE”) was incorporated in Hong Kong on 2 September 2014. The Company develops microbial formulations and organic preparations for agriculture, animal care and organic resource recovery.

SOBE’s focus is on matching microbial and organic preparations to defined applications and suitable product formats. The original range uses liquid, powder and granular formats: powdered nutritional supplements for dogs and cats, bamboo-grade granular fertiliser, liquid fertilisers, and a powder for composting food waste.

Product developmentSelect a formulation and physical format around the intended user, setting and handling requirements.
Partner supportDefine the information distributors need for product selection, label review, storage and local market checks.

In 2018, SOBE presented its microbial waste-treatment solutions at Eco Expo Asia alongside its plant and pet product lines.

03 / Applications

Where the portfolio is used

Six application areas, from crop nutrition to the management of organic waste and water systems.

Agriculture

Crop nutrition, cultivated soils, and root, leaf and seed applications.

Pet care

Pet nutrition, grooming and care for animal spaces.

Livestock

Feed preparations by animal group and livestock housing care.

Resource recovery

Managed composting, bokashi and handling of segregated organic waste.

Water systems

Septic maintenance, managed wastewater and aquaculture pond applications.

Household care

Microbial cleaning for compatible household surfaces.
